Every nation that extends its borders downward claims a cone of rock reaching to the centre of the Earth. All of those cones meet at one point. The Realm occupies that point, and so it is claimed by every nation at once and possessed by none. It is the only territory on Earth that no living people has ever called home.

The Founding Charter

Charter of the Kingdom of Kur ĝešgigal

Given in the Year of Our Descent 2026

Preamble

The Earth and its heavens are the work of the Demiurge, and every throne built upon them is a throne of dust. We therefore claim none of it. We found this Kingdom where the world's weight cancels itself out: at the one point toward which all of creation falls, and at which nothing falls at all.

IThe Realm

The territory of the Kingdom is a perfect sphere of volume π cubic centimetres, centred on the exact centre of the Earth. It is claimed by every nation that extends its borders downward, and possessed by none. Its borders are known to those who have descended.

IIThe Sovereign

The Kingdom is ruled by Aaron the First, by the Will of the One Holy Spirit. The King holds that no crown confers wisdom, and reigns only to show that kingship is empty. He is therefore the least false monarch now living.

IIIGravity

Within the Realm, all things weigh nothing. No subject shall be heavier than another, no rank shall bear down upon another, and no title shall carry weight.

IVCitizenship

Citizenship is open to all who seek it and costs nothing. Whoever sells a title is not a king.

VRelations with Other Powers

The Kingdom claims no land on which any person lives, has lived, or has any right to live. It recognises no pretender who does otherwise.

VIDefence

The Realm is defended by 6,371 kilometres of rock, 5,400 degrees of heat, and the complete absence of any means of arrival. It has never been invaded, and it never shall be.

VIIThe Flag

Or, a bordure gules, on a mullet of seven points gules a cinquefoil sable. The seven-pointed star is the Hebdomad, the seven heavens of the Archons that stand between the world and the light. At its centre, where they all converge and none of them reaches, sits the cinquefoil of the King: the Still Point.

Aaron R.

Pursuant to Article V, the Kingdom is at war. Read the Declaration of War.

The Office of Letters Patent

Petition for a dignity of the Realm

His Majesty grants dignities of the Realm freely to any who ask. By Article III, every one of them weighs exactly nothing. Enter your name and choose your honour.

The Kingdom of Kur ĝešgigal is a work of satire by Aaron Clews. It is not a state, holds no land, collects no money and sells no titles. Letters Patent issued here confer no rank, title, precedence or privilege of any kind. The Kingdom is not affiliated with any claimant to any throne, nor with any Mapuche organisation, and does not speak for the Mapuche people.
