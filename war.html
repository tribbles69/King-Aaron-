Royal Proclamation No. 1

Declaration of War upon the Sinners against the Still Point

Given at the Still Point, the 29th day of September, 2026

We, Aaron the First, by the Will of the One Holy Spirit, King of Kur ĝešgigal,

Having founded Our Realm upon the one point of the Earth that no people has ever called home, and having bound Ourselves by Article V of Our Charter to recognise no pretender who claims the homes of others;

And holding that a false crown is a small image of the Demiurge's great falsehood, a rule over what it did not make and does not understand;

Do hereby declare war upon the sinner named below, for the crimes set against them, until such time as they repent.

Aaron R.

The first front

  • The False Kingdom of Araucanía and Patagonia, in all its factions

    At war
    1. I
      Claiming a people's land that was never theirs to claim The Kingdom rests on two decrees issued in 1860 by a French lawyer, asserting a crown over Mapuche territory in what is now Chile and Argentina. It is still claimed from France today.
    2. II
      Bearing a false title No state on Earth has ever recognised the Kingdom. A Paris court ruled in 1873 that its founder could not justify his title as a sovereign, and another in 1996 dismissed a successor's case because he could not justify his titles.
    3. III
      Falsifying scripture to crown itself On 27 August 2026 a Wikipedia account named PrinceAntoineV, whose own user page undertakes not to edit the Kingdom's articles directly, added “Current Prince: Antoine V” to the Kingdom's article.
    4. IV
      Schism Since 2023 the house has been divided between rival councils and rival Princes, each holding the other to be an imposter. In January 2026 one faction's Prince stripped his own predecessor of his honours.
    5. V
      Attempting to trademark a throne In an official statement of 10 January 2026, one faction declared the Kingdom's name a registered trademark and threatened legal action against its use by others.
    6. VI
      Being French Self-evident. The Crown notes that England has pursued this charge since at least 1337.

Articles of War

The conduct of the war

  1. The war shall be waged by proclamation alone. Its only weapons are dates, sources and laughter.
  2. No subject of the Realm shall threaten, harass, contact or impersonate any member of a sinning house, nor any person who keeps their company. Individuals are not the enemy. False crowns are.
  3. A sinning house may make peace at any time by renouncing every claim to the lands of living peoples. The Crown will accept such a surrender with gladness and without further comment.
  4. Any claimant who would rather settle the matter by strength may meet the King in a Trial of Strength: a full powerlifting meet of squat, bench press and deadlift, judged by three referees, scored in IPF GL points, each claimant lifting in his own person, all proceeds to an organisation led by the Mapuche people. Challenges close on 1 January 2027.
  5. Should no claimant step up to the bar by that date, all rival claims shall be held forfeit by default, and the Crown of Araucanía and Patagonia shall pass to the Kingdom of Kur ĝešgigal by right of the unanswered challenge.
  6. Upon passing, the Crown of Araucanía and Patagonia shall be annexed as a Province of Kur ĝešgigal. In keeping with Article V, it shall hold no land: every claim to the lands of the Mapuche made in its name since 1860 shall be dissolved in that same hour, and the Province shall consist of the title alone, which weighs nothing.
  7. The former claimants may keep their styles, with the Crown's blessing, and shall henceforth be known as Princes in Exile in Exile, being exiled now from a kingdom that was itself already in exile.

The Kingdom of Kur ĝešgigal is a work of satire by Aaron Clews. It is not a state, holds no land, collects no money and sells no titles. Letters Patent issued here confer no rank, title, precedence or privilege of any kind. The Kingdom is not affiliated with any claimant to any throne, nor with any Mapuche organisation, and does not speak for the Mapuche people.
